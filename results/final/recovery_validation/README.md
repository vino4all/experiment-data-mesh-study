# Recovery Validation Results

**Evidence status: SUPPORTED REPLICATED EVIDENCE**

These files contain ten reset-and-reseed recovery trials with a 10000-event
backlog. They support replicated claims about projection catch-up duration and
catch-up rate under the archived local Docker environment.

## Files

- `recovery_trials.csv`: trial-level backlog size, recovery duration, projection
  lag, stale-read rate, and catch-up rate.
- `recovery_summary.csv`: mean, median, standard deviation, and 95 percent
  confidence intervals.
- `recovery_duration_distribution.png`: recovery-duration distribution figure.

## Provenance

Generated by `scripts/run-final-validation-experiments.js`, experiment C. The
runner truncates experiment tables, reseeds deterministic synthetic records,
pauses and resumes the projection consumer, publishes updates, and polls backlog
state until convergence.
